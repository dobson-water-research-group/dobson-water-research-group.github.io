## Policy

- **National water resources planning (2019–2020).** National water-supply modelling from
  Barnaby's time at the University of Oxford formed a core part of the Environment Agency's
  [national framework for water resources](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/meeting-our-future-water-needs-a-national-framework-for-water-resources),
  and became the main tool for bringing together water companies' modelling results to plan
  transfers between them. The Environment Agency recognised the work, including its contribution to
  the National Infrastructure Commission, with a letter of thanks, and Oxford's School of Geography
  recognised it as a REF impact case study.

## Industry and government

- **WSIMOD for public bodies (2022–2024).** Seven commissioned studies for the Environment Agency,
  the Greater London Authority and the Greater Manchester Combined Authority, modelling integrated
  water management across England and Wales.
- **SWMManywhere in Florida (2025).** Arup is using SWMManywhere for The Nature Conservancy to model
  urban drainage in Florida, with Barnaby advising.
- **Only Rainwater in Windermere (2026).** Independent assurer for the project, led by Jacobs and
  Mott MacDonald.
- **Training.** Integrated water modelling workshops for the University of Belgrade (2021),
  Mott MacDonald (2022) and Lund University (2024).

## Open-source software

[WSIMOD](/software/wsimod/) and [SWMManywhere](/software/swmmanywhere/) have been downloaded more
than 28,000 times from PyPI.
