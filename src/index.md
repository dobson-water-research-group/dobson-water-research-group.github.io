We build open-source models that treat the water cycle as one connected system: supply,
wastewater, drainage, rivers and catchments. We use them with utilities, regulators and planners
to ask where interventions will do the most good for water quantity and quality.

## Research

- **Integrated water systems:** linking the parts of the water cycle that are usually modelled separately
- **Urban drainage:** synthetic sewer models from open data, and what drives sewer overflows
- **Water quality:** wastewater, nature-based solutions and river health at catchment scale
- **Decisions under uncertainty:** how modelling choices change the answers we give planners
